How to use the card scanner

Scan Magic cards with your phone to build a price-tracked library. Everything you add is stored on your own phone only — nobody else can see it.

First time

  • Open this page on your phone and tap Allow when it asks for the camera (and the microphone, if you want voice).
  • Tap the share icon and Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.
  • If the picture is blurry, tap Camera to switch to the main lens, and use the Zoom slider so the card fills the frame from about a foot away.

Scanning a card

  • Hold the card so its name sits above the dashed line, then tap Scan card.
  • It shows the card and its market price. Tap Add to save it, or Clear and try again.
  • Auto-scan reads on its own — just swap cards in and out of the frame.
  • Tick Add automatically to save each card the moment it's recognized (you'll hear a beep). Leave it off for valuable cards so you can confirm the exact printing first.

Voice

  • Tap Voice and say a card name — often faster than the camera. Say "undo that" to remove the last one.

Decks & sideboards

  • Above the camera, pick a deck (or + New deck…) before scanning. Everything you scan goes into that deck.
  • The Main deck / Sideboard button sets which pile you're adding to. By voice, say "sideboard" or "main deck".
  • In the Library tab you can filter and group by deck, and move cards between decks.

Your library

  • Filter, sort, and group your cards, and tap Refresh prices to update values.
  • Tap any card to see it full size and fix its printing, foil, quantity, or deck.
  • Export to Excel (or CSV) saves the whole collection to a spreadsheet.
  • Your library lives only in this phone's browser. Tap Backup now and then, and don't clear the browser's site data or you'll lose it. Use Restore to bring a backup back.

What it does behind the scenes

  • It doesn't need a perfect read. Whatever the camera reads is compared against the name of every Magic card ever printed, and the closest match wins. If nothing is close enough, it skips the card instead of guessing.
  • It keeps checking after the beep. The card is saved the instant the name is recognized, so you can move on. In the background it then reads the small print in the card's bottom-left corner (set code and collector number) and, if that points to a different printing, quietly swaps the saved card to the right version.
  • That version check has limits. It only works on cards from about 2014 on, which have that corner print, and only when the print is readable. Otherwise the card goes in as a default printing. Those cards are marked ⚠ check printing as you scan and ⚠ CHECK in the Library. Tap ⚠ Needs a closer look in the Library to list them, then tap a card to pick the printing by hand, Rescan it with the camera, or mark it Looks right. Cards with only one printing are never flagged.
  • It can't see foil. Every scan is saved as non-foil. Tick Foil before adding, or fix it later from the Library.
  • Auto-scan watches for the swap. It waits until a new card is in the frame and held still, reads it once, then waits for the next card, so one card isn't counted twice. For a second copy of the same card, take it out and put it back.
  • Repeat cards are quicker. A card it has already looked up this session doesn't need to be looked up again.
  • Prices are market prices from Scryfall, matched to the exact printing and foil setting. They're saved when you scan; Refresh prices updates the whole library.
  • The same card can live in several places. Copies in different decks, in a sideboard, or in foil are tracked separately, and the header shows the total value and count across all of them.
  • Everyone's library is separate. Friends using the same link get their own empty library on their own phone. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
